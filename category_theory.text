Category-Theoretic Diagram for the Tensor-Algebra Viewpoint
===========================================================

Objects
-------
V = tensor space for a modality
x in V = data tensor
T : V -> V = tensor morphism / endomorphism
E : V -> V = Encrypter
D : V -> V = Decrypter

Core tensor picture
-------------------

              T
    x -----------------> T(x)
    |                     ^
    | E                   |
    v                     |
   E(x) ------------------
          approximation

Learned reconstruction picture
------------------------------

    x --E--> E(x) --D--> D(E(x))

with intended relationships

    E(x) ~= T(x)
    D(E(x)) ~= x

Interpretation
--------------
- V is a tensor space for a modality.
- For images, x can be an RGBA tensor in [0,1]^(H x W x 4).
- For audio, x can be an audio tensor in an analogous tensor space.
- T acts on the whole tensor object, not coordinate-by-coordinate through a separate grid object.
- E is the learned forward model that approximates the tensor morphism T.
- D is the learned reverse model that maps the encrypted tensor back toward the original tensor.

Multimodal framing
------------------
- The current working case in the repository is image tensors.
- The same tensor-algebra viewpoint is intended to extend to audio next.
- The category-theoretic point is that the same kind of endomorphism T : V -> V can be studied on different modality-specific tensor spaces V.
